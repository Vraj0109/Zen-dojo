Interviewer guide, 45 minutes

Merge Intervals

ClarifyApproachCodeTestWrap-up

Say

  • Read the problem as written. Then ask: "Can you say it back in your own words?"
  • Do not mention sorting. Let them find it.

Hold back until asked

  • Input size: up to 10,000 intervals.
  • Touching intervals: [1,4] and [4,5] count as overlapping.
  • Unsorted input: yes, the list is not sorted.

Hint ladder, smallest first

  1. What would make overlapping intervals sit next to each other?
  2. After sorting, what do you compare with the last merged interval?
  3. What is the end of the merged interval when one contains the other?

Give one hint only after two quiet minutes. Note it on the score sheet.

Listen for

Strong: sorts by start and says why; handles the contained case with max of ends; states O(n log n).

Weak: compares every pair; forgets the contained case; no complexity.

Follow-ups

  • The intervals arrive one at a time. What changes?
  • Return the total length covered, not the merged list.
