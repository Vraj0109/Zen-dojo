Shortcuts used

1 day is about 10^5 seconds. A year is about 400 days when you want an easy multiplier; 365 when you want to be exact.

1 million MB is 1 TB, so 60M x 3 MB is 180 million MB, which is 180 TB.

Assumptions

None needed here: every number is given. Other drills ask you to choose the peak-to-average ratio or the object size, and say why a value is reasonable.

Quick-fire, question 4 of 10

How long does it take to read 1 MB sequentially from an SSD?

Right. About 1 ms. A modern SSD reads sequentially at 1 GB per second or more, so 1 MB takes around a millisecond. Hardware varies; this is a rule of thumb. Checked 21 Nov 2026.
