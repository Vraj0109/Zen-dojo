Dynamo: a highly available key-value store

Paper, 2007

Dynamo is the design of a key-value store that stays writable when machines or links fail. It trades strict consistency for availability. Interviewers expect you to name its three main tools: consistent hashing, quorum reads and writes, and version vectors.

What to know

1

The problem and the setting. A shopping cart must accept adds even if a data center is unreachable.

2

The core idea. Accept writes on any replica, keep several versions, and reconcile on read.

3

How it works. A ring of nodes, N replicas per key, R and W counts for reads and writes.

4

Trade-offs and limits. The client may see conflicting versions and must merge them.

5

In interviews. Use it for a key-value store design and any "available during a partition" answer.
