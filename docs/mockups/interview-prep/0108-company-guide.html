The loop, stage by stage

  1. 1. Recruiter call20 to 30 min

    Role, level, location, your timeline. Ask what the next steps are.

  2. 2. Online assessmentabout 2 hours

    Coding problems, often a work-style section. Practise it in the OA simulation.

    Open OA practice
  3. 3. Phone screen45 to 60 min

    One coding problem plus a leadership principle question.

  4. 4. Final loop4 to 5 interviews

    Coding, system design, low-level design, and several rounds where principles are tested. One interviewer is chosen to hold the bar for the whole loop.

  5. 5. Debrief and offerdays to 2 weeks

    Interviewers compare notes and decide together.

Common mistakes

  • Telling "we" stories with no clear personal action.
  • Using one story for every principle question.
  • Starting to code before agreeing on the problem.
