Prepwise: name and logo options

Original marks (no use of any anime's symbols). Each is a flat SVG that works as a tab icon, sidebar logo and app icon.

SHURIKEN
手裏剣
A four-point throwing star. Sharp, recognisable at 16 px. "Sharpen your skills."
KUNAI
苦無
A ninja's all-purpose tool: dig, climb, throw. Matches an app that does a bit of everything.
RONIN
浪人
A samurai with no master: you teach yourself, offline, with nobody's cloud. A brush-ring (enso) cut by one slash.
SHINOBI
忍
A headband plate with code brackets and a slash. "Shinobi" is the word for a ninja. Says "engineering" at a glance.
KAGE
影
"Shadow". Fits a private, offline tool that works quietly in the background. A mask with two red eye-slashes.
DOJO
道場
"Place of the way": where you train every day. A red sun (hinomaru) with a P and a sword stroke. Friendly and learner-focused.
