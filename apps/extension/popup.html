HabitManager · LeetCode

Import your history

For the problems on HabitManager that you've solved (roadmap + other synced ones), saves every accepted submission (code, language, runtime, memory, time, notes) from leetcode.com while you're logged in. Keep the leetcode.com tab open until it finishes. Running it again only fetches what's new.

Live capture

While a leetcode.com tab is open, each Accepted submission is sent to the app automatically.

Time on problems

Counts the minutes you spend on a LeetCode problem page (tab in front, you are moving the mouse or typing). Only the problem name and a number of seconds are sent to your app, never page content.
