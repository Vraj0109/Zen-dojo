Zen Dojo · LeetCode

Save your history to a file

Reads every problem you've solved on leetcode.com while you're logged in and saves every accepted submission (code, language, runtime, memory, time, notes) as zen-dojo-leetcode-<date>.json. Then open Zen Dojo → Profile → Settings and upload the file. Nothing is sent to the app from here. Keep the leetcode.com tab open until it finishes.

Send to the app directly (optional)

Import your history

For the problems on Zen Dojo that you've solved (roadmap + other synced ones), sends every accepted submission straight to the app. Running it again only fetches what's new.

Live capture

While a leetcode.com tab is open, each Accepted submission is sent to the app automatically.

Time on problems

Counts the minutes you spend on a LeetCode problem page (tab in front, you are moving the mouse or typing). Only the problem name and a number of seconds are sent to your app, never page content.
